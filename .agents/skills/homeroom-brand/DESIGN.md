## Typography

As our primary typeface, STK Bureau Serif introduces precision, scientific clarity, and computational restraint into Homeroom’s identity. Its engineered serif structure bridges research credibility with modern systems thinking, moving away from the conventional biotech aesthetic of overly clinical sans serifs or speculative futurism.

As our supporting typeface, Geist brings clarity, neutrality, and systems level readability to Homeroom’s communication layer. Its structured geometry and technical precision complement the sophistication of STK Bureau Serif, creating a balanced typographic system that feels both scientific and operational.

STK Bureau is a licensed font, so the samples on this page are set in Source Serif 4 until the licensed file is added.

- Headlines: **Source Serif 4**, weight 460, tight tracking (-3.5%), short lines.
- Body, labels and UI: **Geist**, weight 400.
- Primary: STK Bureau Serif, Headlines and big statements.
- Secondary: Geist Regular, Body copy, descriptions and interface text.
- Secondary: Geist Medium, Labels, buttons and small headings.

Fonts are in `assets/fonts/`.

## Layout

- Generous margins and lots of empty space.
- Posts: a big headline in Source Serif 4, a picture or the brand's shape, the logo small in a corner, and `www.homeroom.app`.

## Logo



- **Backgrounds:** Black on yellow, cream on black, or in chrome.
- **App icon:** The H mark on a rounded square: cream on black, or black on cream.
- **Sizes:** The wordmark at 196, 144, 96, 60 and 32 pt.
- Use the real logo files. Don't redraw, recolour outside the palette, stretch, outline or add effects.

## Voice

- Smart without sounding smart
- We are: Conversational, Confident, Playful, Simple, Inviting.
- We aren't: Corporate, Hype-y, Silly, Technical, Instructional.
- Say "Come build with us". Don't say "Leverage our collaborative infrastructure".

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do: the palette above, Source Serif 4 headlines, the real logo and pictures.
- Don't: other fonts, colours outside the palette, stock "AI" looks, invented numbers, customers or features.
