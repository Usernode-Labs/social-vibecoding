# Homeroom: voice

## In one line
AI made everyone a builder. Homeroom makes building multiplayer.

## Voice
Smart without sounding smart

We are: Conversational, Confident, Playful, Simple, Inviting.
We aren't: Corporate, Hype-y, Silly, Technical, Instructional.

| We say | We never say |
| --- | --- |
| Come build with us | Leverage our collaborative infrastructure |

## How to write
- Short sentences, in the brand's voice above.
- Specific beats grand. No hype words.
- Never invent stats, customer names, prices or features. If a fact isn't given, leave it out or ask.

## Lines from the brand's own work
- Stop building for people. Build with them.
- Introducing Community Voting
- The room is open for everyone
- Help build it. Get credit for it.
