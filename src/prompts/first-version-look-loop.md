This is the app's first version, so how it looks is worth more of your time than the usual quick check: for this build, the in-loop browser's limit of a couple of check-and-fix cycles and a minute or two does not apply. Once the main screen works, review it in rounds. In each round, boot the app and take screenshots (`browser_take_screenshot`) of the main screen at 390x844 and at 1280x800, in the light and the dark look (`?un-theme=light`, then `?un-theme=dark`), and of its empty and error states; if you read text only, walk the same screens with `browser_snapshot` instead. Compare what you see with your design plan and the skill's self-critique, name the two or three most visible problems, fix them, and start the next round. Stop after five rounds or about fifteen minutes, or sooner once a round finds nothing worth fixing, and finish well inside the build's own clock.
