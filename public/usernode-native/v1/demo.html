Kit demo & tuning page. Add ?un-platform=ios|android to force a skin, ?un-tune=1 for the spring tuner. Best judged on a real phone.

Buttons — pressed states

No tap flash; press engages instantly, release springs back.

Switches

Swipe to act (drag a row left)

Partial swipe reveals actions; a full swipe (or a hard flick) deletes. Catch a springing row mid-motion — it follows your finger from where it is.

Inset-grouped list (swipe a row left)

Grouped rows — rounded, with margins

The action tray rides along with the row — no corner fringe on the rounded card, and the inset hairline separators hold still during the swipe.

Settings-style rows

Sheets & dialogs

Sheet: drag it down (or flick) to dismiss — catch it mid-spring. Action sheet & alert resolve a Promise with your choice.

Pull to refresh (touch devices)

Spinner grows with the pull — release past the line (or yank) to refresh; a new row lands after ~1s. The PAGE itself also refreshes: scroll to the very top and pull the whole page down (window-scroller mode).

Page transitions & nav bar

iOS: slide + parallax. Android: shared-axis fade. Old browsers: instant cut. Screen B shows the blurred nav bar with a collapsing large title.
