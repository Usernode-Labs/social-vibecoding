Kit demo & tuning page. Add ?un-platform=ios|android to force a skin, ?un-tune=1 for the spring tuner. Best judged on a real phone.

Buttons — pressed states

No tap flash; press engages instantly, release springs back.

Hit areas — .un-touch-target

A 34px clear button pinned inside the field with absolute, carrying .un-touch-target directly — no wrapper. Taps land up to ~5px outside the visible circle.

Partial swipe reveals actions; a full swipe (or a hard flick) deletes. Catch a springing row mid-motion — it follows your finger from where it is.

Grab a ≡ handle (long-press the row on touch) — the row lifts and follows 1:1, an accent bar marks the drop slot, edges auto-scroll, release springs it into place. Hovering a section header inserts at the top of that section.

Swipe AND reorder (long-press to lift)

Grid reorder (homescreen displacement)

grid: true: long-press (or drag in any direction on desktop) lifts a floating ghost that tracks the pointer on both axes; the tiles slide aside and a dashed box marks the exact cell you'd drop into — like moving an icon on a phone home screen.

Sheets & dialogs

Sheet: drag it down (or flick) to dismiss — catch it mid-spring. On a phone, tap the sheet's text field: the sheet (and the modal/alert) rides above the on-screen keyboard. Action sheet & alert resolve a Promise with your choice. Modal: centered card, backdrop tap or Escape dismisses. ?un-demo=action-sheet (and ?un-demo=adaptive-menu) opens the touch menu idiom at load.

Side panels

The nav-drawer idiom: full height, springs in from the edge over the dimmed page. Deliberately NOT draggable — a drawer is opened by a control and closed by the backdrop, Escape, or picking a row; drag-to-dismiss and its grabber pill belong to the bottom sheet. "Long content" scrolls normally inside the panel. Tap the text field: the content lifts clear of the on-screen keyboard. ?un-demo=panel opens the right panel at load.

Popover / dropdown menu

The desktop menu idiom: opens attached to its button, flipped/clamped to stay in the viewport, dismissed by outside-click / Escape / scroll — click the button again to toggle it closed. Arrow keys walk the entries. "Edge clamp/flip" sits at the right edge (and flips above when there's no room below — scroll it near the bottom). "Adaptive menu" is ONE unNative.menu() call: a bottom action sheet on touch (?un-platform=ios), this popover on desktop. "Icon menu" adds icon: to the rows — a name from the kit's set, or iconEl for your own node; a row without one is still aligned, and the glyph inherits the row's colour, so the destructive row's icon is red.

Toasts

iOS/desktop: bottom capsule. Android: snackbar. Tap "Toast" repeatedly — a new toast replaces the old one (last-writer-wins). A priority toast holds the slot: fire "Undo flow", then tap "Toast" while the Undo is up — it waits its turn and appears after the undo window resolves instead of eating the Undo button.

Pull to refresh (touch devices)

Spinner grows with the pull — release past the line (or yank) to refresh; a new row lands after ~1s. The PAGE itself also refreshes: scroll to the very top and pull the whole page down (window-scroller mode).

Page transitions & nav bar

iOS: slide + parallax. Android: shared-axis fade. Old browsers: instant cut. Screen B shows the blurred nav bar with a collapsing large title.

Zoom from element (iOS homescreen)

Tap a tile: Screen D expands out of its on-screen rect (unNative.transition(fn, {type:'zoom-in', el, fromEl})) while this grid stays visible beneath, and Back shrinks it into the same tile (type:'zoom-out'). The LIVE element animates — no View Transition snapshot, so embedded content keeps loading mid-zoom. When the zoom can't run (tile off-screen / deep link / reduced motion) it falls back to push/pop.

Keyboard avoidance (fixed shell)

The native-app shell: the page frame never scrolls — one position:fixed; inset:0 pane holds the content, and attachKeyboardAvoidance owns the keyboard. On a phone: tap the bottom field — the content slides ONCE, no double motion; hop between fields — each is revealed immediately; dismiss the keyboard — the clearance eases away instead of snapping. Pull down to refresh on the same pane (composition with element-mode PTR).
