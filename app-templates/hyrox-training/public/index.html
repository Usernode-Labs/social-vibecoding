A Hyrox race is eight 1 km runs with a station after each. Train the stations, the run, or the whole thing, and every session is logged here.

The race

Run 1 1 km
Station 1
SkiErg 1000 m on the ski erg
Run 2 1 km
Station 2
Sled Push Push the sled the length of the lane
Run 3 1 km
Station 3
Sled Pull Pull the sled back, hand over hand on the rope
Run 4 1 km
Station 4
Burpee Broad Jumps A burpee, then a broad jump, again and again
Run 5 1 km
Station 5
Rowing 1000 m on the rower
Run 6 1 km
Station 6
Farmers Carry A heavy weight in each hand, carried the length of the lane
Run 7 1 km
Station 7
Sandbag Lunges Lunges with the sandbag on your shoulders
Run 8 1 km
Station 8
Wall Balls Wall balls to the target until the count is done

Open and Pro divisions differ in the sleds' weights, the carry, the lunges and the wall balls.
