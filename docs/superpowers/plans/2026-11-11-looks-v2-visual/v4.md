## How to read this

- **Content check (step 1) was done by machine as well as by eye.** `textdiff.mjs` listed every visible text
  node and every visible control of the list and of the open section, in the standard look and in Glass, on a
  desktop (1440) and a phone (390), for all 18 steps. Result: **no text and no control of the standard page is
  missing under Glass, and nothing is added**, except the kit's head ("Settings", "Your account, your
  preferences and your tools.") and the section's name at 22px on the pages where the standard look does not
  repeat it. Logs: `local/v-v4/textdiff-d.log`, `textdiff-ph.log`. There is no A finding in this group.
- **The server was overloaded during the first runs** (load average above 100; answers 429 and a
  "Reconnecting to Homeroom. Showing saved content" banner). Several captures show "Could not load…" lines
  for that reason. They are not findings about the look; where one hid the content I captured again. Two
  drawing faults only show in that state and are listed as such (F2, s63-3).
- In a phone's full-page capture the tab bar is painted across the middle of the picture. That is how a fixed
  bar is captured, not a fault.

## Faults that repeat on every step (listed once, counted once)

**F1. B. Phone, every look seen (Glass, Sunrise, Today, Playground, Workbench), light and dark: a long section
name in the bar runs under the bar's buttons.** "Agent instructions & skills" is drawn through the look
picker and the theme button; "AI usage & models", "Linked accounts", "Linked devices", "Connectors & CLI" and
"App permissions" lose their last letters under the look picker; "Welcome tour" and "Notifications" touch it.
Captures: `v-v4-s63-glass-ph.png` (worst), `v-v4-s61-glass-ph.png`, `v-v4-s58-glass-ph.png`,
`v-v4-s60-sunrise-ph-dark.png`, `v-v4-s62-glass-ph.png`, `v-v4-s68-glass-ph-dark.png`.
Source: the crumb's last word is `.fr-ci.fr-here.fr-keep` inside `.fr-crumb` (kit.css lines 33 to 37:
`white-space: nowrap; min-width: 0`, no `overflow: hidden` and no ellipsis). Measured on
`#settings/agent-files` at 390px: the crumb's box is 56 to 139px, its text is 193px wide, the look picker
starts at 155px. The fault is the frame's, so any page with a long name on a phone has it.

**F2. B. Every field's value is drawn in the placeholder's faint ink.** The MCP server URL (s62), the Cap $
amount "1.00" (s68), the Look menu's value (s73) and the Language menu's value (s66) all compute to
`rgb(139, 144, 176)` in Glass, the kit's `--fr-faint`, where the standard look draws them in the text colour.
A filled field cannot be told from an empty one, and the URL a person is meant to copy is the palest thing on
the page. Seen in Glass, Today, Sunrise, Brand kit, Paper, Pastel, Mosaic dark. Captures:
`v-v4-s62-glass.png`, `v-v4-s68-today.png`, `v-v4-s73-glass.png`, `v-v4-s66-paper-ph.png`.
Source: the wear map's `inp` root (looks.json) puts the kit's `.inp` on the product's `input`, `select` and
`textarea` themselves (app.css line 28732). In the kit `.inp` is a wrapper whose own colour is `--faint`, and
the value takes `--ink` from `.inp.val`; the product's element never gets `val`, so its typed value keeps the
wrapper's faint colour. Only `.ui-inp-code` is given ink (line 28739).

**F3. B/C. When the "Reconnecting to Homeroom" banner is up, the frame's bar is half hidden under it.** On a
phone the bar slides under the "Add it to your home screen" strip and only the bottom half of its buttons
shows; on a desktop the banner cuts the top of the bar's buttons. Seen in Glass, Today, Brand kit, Playground.
Captures of the first run, since overwritten for s58; still visible in `v-v4-s62-brandkit-ph.png`,
`v-v4-s63-playground-ph-dark.png`, `v-v4-s59-playground.png`, `v-v4-s62-glass-dark.png`. Not Settings' own
fault (the frame's bar and the shell's offline banner), and only reachable when the connection drops.
