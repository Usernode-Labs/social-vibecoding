## How to read this, and its limits

- **The stack was slow and shared** during the pass (`/api/apps` took 8 to 12 s, sometimes longer). Home's
  apps read gives up on its own timeout, after which the standard page hides Pinned apps and the kit page
  draws nothing for it; Main Chat once showed "Couldn't reach Homeroom … Try again" (`s10-glass-dark`). My
  script asks Home to load again (a read) until the apps arrive. These states are the stack's, not findings.
- "Tall" captures use a tall viewport (2400 to 5200px) instead of scrolling, so a page is seen whole.
- Several captures were read scaled down (a 1440px page at 1000 to 1300px; tall pages lower). Small text
  faults (1 to 2px misalignments, 11px text contrast) can have escaped me there.
- Not every capture taken was studied with the same care. Where a combination is listed as "seen, nothing
  noted" it was looked at once, whole, with no fault standing out.
- Severity: `A` content missing or extra, or page unusable; `B` clearly wrong drawing; `C` polish.

## s01-home

Seen: standard desktop and phone; Glass desktop, phone, dark, 1024px; Paper desktop and phone; Workbench

communities, the directory filtered).

Findings
- **B · Mosaic, desktop.** The Pinned apps card is broken: the twelve tiles are squeezed into the left 290px
  with names cut ("Homeroo", "RecipeBo", "GOAL! Champi…") and "Show all 95 apps" becomes a tall empty card
  filling the right half. `s01-mosaic`. Cause: `frontend/looks/mosaic.css` line 40,
  `.cols>.stack:first-child .card:has(>.row){display:grid;grid-template-columns:repeat(2,…)}`, meant for
  lists of rows, catches this card because its last child is `.row.showall`. On a phone the same rule strips
  the card from the tiles and leaves "Show all 95 apps" as a card of its own (`s01-mosaic-phone`).
- **B · Glass (any look), 1024px wide.** The two columns stay side by side and the tile grid keeps six
  columns in a 350px card: names are cut mid-letter with no ellipsis ("Homeroc", "RecipeBc", "whiteboa",
  "FirstWor", "WorkQue", "Somethir"). `s01-glass-1024`. (`.tiles` in `kit.css`; `home-frame.tsx`.)
- **B · every look, phone.** Next challenges comes after Communities to join, which is a paged directory:
  it is reached only after scrolling through every public community. On the standard phone page it sits
  before the directory. Order measured: standard Pinned apps, Pinned communities, Next challenges,
  Communities to join; Glass Pinned apps, Pinned communities, Communities to join, Next challenges.
  `s02-std-phone-tall`, `s02-glass-phone-tall`, `s01-glass-phone-end`. (The side column stacks last:
  `.cols` in `kit.css`.)
- **C · every look.** The folded grid shows 12 apps (two rows of six) where the standard shows 8 (two rows
  of four), with the same "Show all 95 apps". `s01-std`, `s01-glass`.
- **C · every look.** A Next challenges row has a trophy glyph and no picture and no arrow button; the
  standard card has the challenge's picture, a progress chip and an arrow. The row's text is the standard's
  state line. `s01-std-search` (cards) against `s01-glass`.
- **C · every look.** Community tiles lose the standard's coloured ring that tells a community from an app;
  under a look the two grids' tiles look the same. `s01-std`, `s01-glass`.
- **C · every look, phone.** The search field is always on the page under the title; the standard phone
  page keeps it hidden until pulled (`#home-search-bar[data-revealed="false"]`). `s01-std-phone`,
  `s01-glass-phone`.

Ruled (lane H): the title "Home" and its sentence; no count on app tiles; no dragging to rearrange and no
hold-to-open menu; a challenge row's line is its state label (no description); the waitlist card, the verify
card and the iOS widget strip are not drawn under a look.

Not seen: a locked or failed tile with Retry, the tile menu (right-click), Add other apps' sheet, the
waitlist, verify and widget states, a private member's Home.
