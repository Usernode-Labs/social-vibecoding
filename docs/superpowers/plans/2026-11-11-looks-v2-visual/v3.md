Build seen: `http://localhost:3477`, source `sv-skins` at `5e90072f2` (`redesign/skins-2026-11-11`), 11 Nov 2026.
Signed in as `snait`. Read only: nothing was submitted, no form that writes was sent.

Captures are in `skins-tour-v2/local/caps/`, all named `v-v3-<name>.png` (the names below leave the prefix
out). Helpers, job lists, page-text dumps and my running notes are in `skins-tour-v2/local/v-v3/`
(`cap.mjs` is the capture script; `caps-text/*.txt` are the `innerText` dumps used for the content diffs;
`notes.txt`).

How content was compared: for each step the standard and Glass pages were captured on a desktop and their
visible text was dumped and diffed line by line (navigation removed), then the pictures were read side by side.

Conditions that limit this pass:

- The machine was heavily loaded during the pass (load average 30 to 300, other captures running). Early
  captures of project pages showed "Couldn't load the feed" or bounced to the hub; they were retaken until
  the page was whole. No finding below rests on a capture that had not settled.
- `shot.mjs` printed no console error on any step except: `429` on `/api/ui-telemetry/batch` (many captures,
  the telemetry limiter under the load of several agents; not a page fault), media `500`s on project pages
  (expected), and one `pageerror: TypeError: Network request timed out` on `#apps/puzzlechain-6cf8ff` under
  load (not reproduced on the retake). No horizontal page scroll on any capture (measured on every one).
- Severity: `A` content missing or extra, or page unusable; `B` clearly wrong drawing; `C` polish.
