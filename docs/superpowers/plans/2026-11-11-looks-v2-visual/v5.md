# Visual pass, group v5: signed out (o01 to o27) and the waiting room

Build: `http://localhost:3477` (worktree `sv-skins`, `redesign/skins-2026-11-11` at `5e90072f2`). Lane: G.
Read only; nothing was submitted. Every capture was taken in a fresh browser context with no saved
sign-in (my runner `local/v-v5/run.mjs` never loads `state-3477.json`), and each one records whether the
bar shows Sign in. It did on every page that has a bar for a visitor. The pages with no Sign in button are
the stand-alone ones (o08 to o11, o27), which have none in the standard look either, and the waiting
room, whose bar says Sign out by design.

Captures are `local/caps/v-v5-<name>.png`; the page text of each is `local/v-v5/txt/<name>.txt`.
Name prefixes: `oNN-` plain capture; `pNN-` the same step with Apple, Google and the phone link answered
in the browser (the local copy has no keys, so I answered `/api/public/waitlist/options` in the test
browser only, as lane G did); `t02-`/`t21-` a tall desktop viewport (the desktop page scrolls inside the
frame, so `full` gives 900px); `e02-` scrolled to the end; `w21-`/`d21-` the Workshop and Discussion
tabs of o21; `wr-`/`wri-` the waiting room through the product's own `/?shot=waiting` and
`/?shot=waiting-invite` fixtures; `o27x-` extra looks for o27.

Two things about the run:

- **I hit the server's rate limit once.** My first batch ran six pages at a time and the server answered
  429 on Game Corner for about a minute (five o21 captures were lost and retaken at three at a time).
  Anyone else capturing from this machine during that minute may have seen 429 too.
- `AuthScreens.showWaiting()` signed out goes straight to `#login` on this build (the room's own check
  of `/api/auth/me` answers 401), so the waiting room was seen through `?shot=waiting` instead.

Content was compared two ways: by eye, and by diffing the visible text of the standard and Glass
captures. On every step the text is the same apart from letter case of captions and the frame's
wordmark, except where a finding says otherwise.
