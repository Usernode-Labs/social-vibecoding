This privacy notice (“Privacy Notice”) explains the basis for our collection of personal data when you use our website https://onhomeroom.com/ (“Website”), the web application at app.onhomeroom.com (“Web Application”) or our app “Homeroom”(Apple Store) / “Homeroom: Apps with friends” (Google Play) (“App”, collectively “Platform” or “Homeroom”), when you use our services, when you communicate with us or when you otherwise deal with us, and sets out how we use your personal data, the conditions under which we disclose it to others and the measures we take to keep it secure. In addition, we may inform you about the processing of your personal data separately, for example in consent forms, terms and conditions, additional privacy notices, forms, and other notices. We use the word “data” here interchangeably with “personal data”.

If you provide information to us about you or any person other than yourself, you must ensure that the data is accurate and that these other people understand how their data will be used, that they have given their permission for you to disclose it to us and for you to allow us, and our service providers, to use it. You are welcome to provide them with a copy of this Privacy Notice.

This Privacy Notice is aligned with the Federal Data Protection Act (“FDPA”) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”). However, the application of these laws depends on each individual case.

## 1. Controller

The responsible person for processing your data under this Privacy Notice (“Controller”) unless we tell you otherwise in an individual case is:

Usernode Labs AG, Bahnhofstrasse 7, 6300 Zug, Switzerland

You may contact us regarding data protection matters and to exercise your rights at: dataprotection@onhomeroom.com.

## 2. Data Collection and Purpose of Data Processing

We process the following data about you for the purposes outlined below:

### 2.1 Platform

When you visit our Platform, we collect data that is necessary for its functionality and security.

The data collected includes: IP address; information about the operating system of your end device; cookies (Web Application); referrer URL; amount of data transferred; date, region, and time of the server request; websites accessed or from which access is made; browser type and version; protocols; cookieless analytics (Website, Web Application, App); etc.

### 2.2 Waitlist

If you subscribe to our waitlist, we process the information provided by you in order to provide you with your invitation and access code as well as to inform you about the Homeroom release. You can unsubscribe from our waitlist by using the subscription link within the emails or by sending us an email to the address mentioned in Section 2.

The data collected includes: email address; IP address; time of sign-up; inviter / referral information; invitation confirmation and release notification; access code; optional survey answers (how the person found us, country, project URL and notes, group details, self-reported social handles); information in connection with the optional verification via GitHub, X/Twitter or LinkedIn (account identifier and handle, display names).

### 2.3 Newsletter

If you choose to subscribe to our newsletters, we process the information provided by you in order to send you product news and other updates about our services. Subscription to the newsletter is voluntary. You can unsubscribe from the newsletters at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in each newsletter or by sending us an email to the address mentioned in Section 2.

The data collected includes: email address; information about your newsletter subscriptions and interests; information about your interaction with our newsletters.

### 2.4 Account

Our Platform can only be accessed after you have successfully finished a registration process and opened an account. To access our Platform, you must visit our Website or download and install the App on your mobile device and create an account. You may also choose to verify your account. Account verification is optional and is not required to access the Platform. We process the data collected in this context in order to provide access to our Platform and to ensure its functionality and security.

The data collected includes: username; hashed password; email address (and confirmation status); display name; push token (stored encrypted) with platform and permission status; wallet address; phone number (for phone sign-in via Google Firebase); sign-in with Apple / Google (provider identifier and email); verified GitHub and X account links (identifier and handle); optional identity verification (verification result, nationality, unique identifier (nullifier)); profile picture; biography and language setting; username history; referral / invitation information; added API keys for AI providers (Anthropic, OpenRouter); information regarding your consent to the Terms of Service; your acknowledgement of this Privacy Notice; etc.

### 2.5 Software Development **(changed in 1.1)**

Our Platform provides a technical development environment that enables users to collaboratively create, develop and manage applications. The Platform offers tools that allow users to communicate, contribute content and code, manage permissions, participate in governance processes and vote on development decisions. We process personal data as necessary to provide, maintain and secure this development environment.

The data collected includes: account and profile data; feedback, proposals, requests and specifications; comments and chat messages; votes and kudos; conversations with the AI coding assistant; time spent in each app; AI usage and cost records (without content); application configuration data and technical usage data generated through the use of the development environment; etc.

**Screen usage data.** When you use the Web Application or the App, we also record how you move through it, linked to your account: which screens you open and in what order, how long pages take to load, and which actions fail. We do not record what you type or what is shown on your screen. We use this data to understand where people get stuck and to fix it, for example to see how many people who are let in from the waitlist finish signing up. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not give it to third parties for their own purposes.

We record screen usage data on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding how the Platform is used and in improving it (see Section 3). You can object at any time by emailing dataprotection@onhomeroom.com. We will then delete the screen usage data recorded about you and stop recording it. Your account, your votes, messages and projects, and the record of which apps you use each day are not affected, because the Platform runs on them.

We keep screen usage data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the detail of each record is erased and what remains is no longer linked to you.

### 2.6 Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

We process your data when you use AI enabled functions provided through our Platform. We use third party AI models and services, made available through OpenRouter and Anthropic. The available models and providers may change from time to time, and users may, where available, select a model or connect their own AI account or API key.

This processing serves to provide AI functions, generate requested responses and outputs, review code against applicable content rules, ensure the proper functioning and security of the Platform, and test, debug and improve our services. We do not operate or train our own AI models. Where AI services are provided through our accounts, we configure them so that inputs and outputs are not used for model training. Where users connect their own AI services, they are responsible for their use, including the respective settings, configurations and the respective terms of service apply.

The data collected includes: account information; prompts, instructions, files and other content submitted to AI functions; AI generated responses and outputs; model and usage information; and technical data. AI conversations may be stored to allow users to continue them and are deleted with the relevant account. We do not routinely review prompts or outputs, except where content is reported or subject to automated content review.

### 2.7 Public and Private Application

Once an application (private or public) is made available, the users involved in developing the application are responsible for the application and the processing of personal data through the application. To the extent Usernode processes personal data on behalf of these users, Usernode acts as a processor. Accordingly, the processing of personal data through the application is not governed by this Privacy Notice.

### 2.8 Messaging Service

We process your data when you use the messaging service provided within our Platform, such as messaging other users who accessed the Website or have installed the App. This processing serves to enable and maintain communication between users, ensure the proper functioning and security of the communication channel, and, where necessary, prevent misuse or technical issues.

The data collected includes: information regarding the sender; message content and replies; conversation and its members; timestamps (sent, edited, deleted); read status (read position per member); attachments (file content, name, type, size); edit / deletion markers; reactions; blocks; reports with evidence snapshots; bookmarks; information regarding push notifications (device installation ID, platform, permission status and encrypted push token); etc.

If you send messages to the Homeroom bot, the content of these messages and related information may be processed by the AI service providers we use to provide the Homeroom functionality.

### 2.9 Running a Node

If you choose to run a Homeroom node through the App, we process certain additional information in order to operate the node functionality, maintain network security, monitor node performance, provide leaderboard functionality, and troubleshoot technical issues.

The data collected includes: email address; community handle; registration code; hashed device identifier; peer ID; wallet address; device and operating system information; battery and power state information; IP address; network and application status information; granted permissions; node performance data (including sync status, blocks produced or missed, slots won, and peer information); crash logs; related diagnostic information; CPU architecture; emulator flag; foreground-service and wake-lock state; app uptime; leaderboard data (peer ID, app version, sync status, block counts, wallet address); etc.

### 2.10 Additional Features

Certain features or functionalities may involve the processing of additional personal data that is not covered by this Privacy Notice. Where this is the case, separate privacy notices or supplemental privacy disclosures will be provided for the relevant feature or functionality. We encourage you to review such notices carefully before using the respective feature, application, or service.

### 2.11 Marketing and Innovation

We process your data for innovation, marketing and business activities in relation to Homeroom, in particular to further develop Homeroom, the Website, the App, and other platforms on which we operate.

### 2.12 Communication

When you contact us by email, telephone, letter, contact form or other means of communication, we collect the data exchanged between you and us for the purposes of communicating with you and providing our services to you, in particular to respond to your enquiries. By providing us with this data, you acknowledge that we use your data in accordance with this Privacy Notice.

The data collected includes: name; email address; topic; content of message; type, manner, place and time of communication; reports, complaints, infringement notices; appeals; etc.

### 2.13 Safety or Security Reasons

We process your data to protect our IT and other infrastructure. For example, we process data for monitoring, analysis and testing of our networks and IT infrastructures including access controls.

### 2.14 Compliance with Law and Legal Procedures

We process your data to comply with legal requirements, and we might have to request further information from you to comply with such requirements or as otherwise required by law and legal authorities. Furthermore, we may process your data for the enforcement of legal claims and for the defence in legal disputes and official proceedings.

### 2.15 Risk Management, Corporate Governance and Business Development

We process your data as part of our risk management and corporate governance in order to protect us from criminal or abusive activity. As part of our business development, we might sell businesses, parts of businesses or companies to others or acquire them from others or enter into partnerships and this might result in the exchange and processing of data based on your consent, if necessary.

### 2.16 Cookies **(changed in 1.1)**

Our Web Application uses cookies. Cookies are text files that are stored on your device (computer, laptop, smartphone, etc.). The cookies used in our Web Application are necessary to provide certain functionalities, in particular to enable and maintain user sessions, authentication and sign-in functionalities, manage access rights and invitations, and protect the Web Application against automated misuse (see Google reCAPTCHA in Section 2.17). One of them tells us whether a browser has opened an invite link before, so that the person who shared the link can see how many different people opened it. Our Web Application does not use cookies for advertising, and it does not use third-party analytics cookies.

You can set your browser to block or disable certain types of cookies or alternative technologies, or to delete existing cookies. Blocking the cookies described above may stop you from signing in. You can find more information on the help pages of your browser (usually with the keyword “Privacy”).

## 3. Legal Basis for Data Processing **(changed in 1.1)**

Insofar as we have asked for your consent, we process your data on the basis of this consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by sending us a written notification (email to: dataprotection@onhomeroom.com ). The withdrawal of your consent affects neither the legality of the processing that we carried out before your withdrawal nor the processing of your data on the basis of other processing grounds.

If we do not need your consent, we will not obtain it and we will process your data for other reasons, such as the initiation/execution of a contract or a business relationship with you, a legal obligation, a vital interest of the data subject or another natural person, or to perform a public task. We may also process your personal data if we have a legitimate interest in doing so, which includes, for example, complying with applicable law and marketing our products and services, the interest in better understanding our markets and in safely and efficiently managing and developing our company, including its activities. This includes recording screen usage data (Section 2.5), which we do on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding how the Platform is used and in improving it. You can object to it at any time (Section 11).

## 5. Disclosure of Data Abroad

The data that we collect from you may be transferred to, processed, and stored in, a country outside the European Economic Area (EEA) or Switzerland. In view of the EEA or Switzerland the law in some of those countries may not offer an adequate level of data protection. We only transfer data to these countries when it is necessary for the performance of a contract or for the exercise or defence of legal claims, or if such transfer is based on your explicit consent or subject to safeguards that assure the protection of your data, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, the CH-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission (SCCs), adjusted according to Swiss law, all of the aforementioned if applicable and required.

## 6. Profiling and Automated Decision Taking

We may use automated processing for the purposes laid out in Section 2 to: (i) calculate contribution points, leaderboard positions and allocation scores, (ii) determine AI usage budgets and eligibility to participate in certain votes based on verified identity status, (iii) automatically review code changes against applicable content rules and, where applicable, block them from being merged, and (iv) analyse usage data to identify instances where users may encounter difficulties when using the application.

This analysis identifies correlations between different behaviours and characteristics to create profiles for individuals. Some of these processing activities may constitute profiling or automated decision taking, depending on their specific implementation and their effects on the individual concerned.

We do not use profiling that can produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you without human review. Where we take a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you, we will inform you accordingly and take the measures required by applicable law.

## 7. On-Chain Data

When you use blockchains, you acknowledge that your wallet address and other data/information provided by your transactions, which are considered personal data if relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, are permanently and publicly stored on-chain, which means such data is publicly available to anyone. Neither we, nor any third party, has any power to delete such data published by its users to the blockchain. If you want to ensure that your privacy rights are not affected in any way, you should not transact on blockchains as certain rights may not be available or exercisable by you or us due to the technological infrastructure of the blockchain.

You hereby release and indemnify us of any liability associated with data that you transferred to the blockchain.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

The Platform is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect or process data from children under this age.

Where the processing of a minor’s data requires consent under applicable data protection laws, such consent must be provided or authorized by the minor’s parent or legal guardian if the minor has not reached the age at which they may independently provide valid consent under the applicable law. Depending on the applicable jurisdiction, parental or legal guardian consent may therefore be required for minors aged 13 or older.

We have implemented measures such as Child Safety Standards on the Platform to protect minors and safeguard their privacy. If we become aware that we have collected or processed a personal data without the consent required by applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address the situation, including deleting the relevant data where necessary. Parents or legal guardians who believe that their child’s data has been processed without the necessary consent may contact us using the contact details provided in this Privacy Notice.

We only process your data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes we collected it for, including for the purposes of complying with legal retention requirements or other legal obligations and where required to assert or defend against legal claims, until the end of the relevant retention period or until the claims in question have been settled. Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, we will securely destroy your data in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

We take appropriate organizational and technical security measures to prevent your data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way, altered, or disclosed. However, we and your data can still become victims of cyber-attacks, cybercrime, brute force, hacker attacks and further fraudulent and malicious activity including but not limited to viruses, forgeries, malfunctions, and interruptions which is out of our control and responsibility. We have also put in place procedures to deal with any suspected data breach and will notify you and any applicable regulator of a breach where we are legally required to do so.
